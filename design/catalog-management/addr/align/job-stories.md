# Catalog Management — Unifying Job Stories

> **Subdomain:** Catalog Management
> **System:** Mechatronic Product Ecommerce (drones, motors, ESCs, flight controllers, sensors, frames, batteries, FPV gear, etc.)
> **Phase:** ADDR — Align

---

## JS1: Product Lifecycle Management

**WHEN** new mechatronic products need to be added to the catalog or existing products need updates,
**I WANT TO** create, update, and manage product records with their specifications, documentation, and lifecycle states,
**SO I CAN** ensure the catalog accurately represents our current product offerings and customers always see correct information.

## JS2: Technical Documentation Management

**WHEN** datasheets, compliance certificates, CAD files, or other technical documents become available or need updating,
**I WANT TO** upload, organize, and associate technical documentation with their respective products,
**SO I CAN** provide customers with the authoritative reference materials they need for evaluation and procurement.

## JS3: Pricing and Promotion Management

**WHEN** pricing strategy, volume discount tiers, or promotional offers need to change,
**I WANT TO** set and adjust product pricing with effective dates and defined rules,
**SO I CAN** implement the business pricing strategy across the catalog while maintaining full price history.

## JS4: Inventory and Availability Management

**WHEN** stock levels, supplier lead times, or availability conditions change,
**I WANT TO** update product availability, lead times, and restock information,
**SO I CAN** ensure customers see accurate availability data and set appropriate delivery expectations.

## JS5: Category and Taxonomy Management

**WHEN** the product catalog needs reorganization, new categories, or product reassignment,
**I WANT TO** manage the category hierarchy, facet definitions, and product-to-category assignments,
**SO I CAN** maintain a well-organized browsing experience that helps customers discover products.

## JS6: Compatibility Rules Management

**WHEN** new products are added or component compatibility knowledge evolves,
**I WANT TO** define and maintain rules specifying which components work together and why,
**SO I CAN** ensure the compatibility verification system provides accurate results and meaningful replacement suggestions.
