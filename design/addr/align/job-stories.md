# Catalog Storefront — Unifying Job Stories

## JS1: Product Discovery
**WHEN** I need to find a mechatronic component or complete drone for my project,
**I WANT TO** search and filter the catalog using technical specifications and categories,
**SO I CAN** quickly identify products that meet my requirements.

## JS2: Product Evaluation
**WHEN** I have found one or more candidate products,
**I WANT TO** view detailed technical specifications, documentation, and related products,
**SO I CAN** determine if a product is the right fit for my application.

## JS3: Product Comparison
**WHEN** I am deciding between multiple similar products,
**I WANT TO** compare their specifications side by side,
**SO I CAN** make an informed purchasing decision based on technical merit.

## JS4: Compatibility Verification
**WHEN** I am building or configuring a drone system,
**I WANT TO** verify that selected components are compatible with each other,
**SO I CAN** avoid ordering parts that won't work together.

## JS5: Catalog Browsing by Category
**WHEN** I am exploring what's available without a specific product in mind,
**I WANT TO** browse the catalog through a structured category hierarchy,
**SO I CAN** discover products and understand the range of options in each category.

## JS6: Partner Catalog Access
**WHEN** I am a partner system that needs current product information,
**I WANT TO** retrieve catalog data including specs, pricing, and availability indicators,
**SO I CAN** display or process accurate product information in my own system.
