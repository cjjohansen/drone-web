    The bought payment service. It takes the card on its own payment sheet and tells Drone Web the outcome by webhook.

    The warehouse side, run from the ERP. It receives PaidOrder and ships the parcel.
    Carrier events foreign
    The carrier that delivers the parcel. It knows the parcel by its tracking number only.
