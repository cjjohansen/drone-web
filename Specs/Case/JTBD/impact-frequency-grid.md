# Impact × Frequency Grid

> *Place roles on the grid based on how much the decisions will create impact and how often they will use the control center.*

---

## Grid Axes

- **Y-Axis (Vertical):** Impact — "What is the Impact on Value / Decision?"
  - HIGH (top) → LOW (bottom)

- **X-Axis (Horizontal):** Frequency
  - LOW (left) → HIGH (right)

## Key Insights

1. **Priority Users** (high impact, high frequency) should drive UX design decisions.
2. **Influencers** need executive dashboards and summary views.
3. **Usability Focus** users need efficient, streamlined workflows.
4. **Inform Only** stakeholders may primarily need read access, notifications, or reports.
