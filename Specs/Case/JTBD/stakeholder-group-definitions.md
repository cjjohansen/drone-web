# Role Clusters - Product Control Center Stakeholders

## Stakeholder Groups Definition

### 1. Governance & Standards (Purple)
**Purpose:** Ensures that product data rules, standards, ownership, and compliance are defined, maintained and enforced across the organization.

**Typical responsibilities:**
- Defining data standards, rules, and compliance
- Clarifying ownership of attributes and domains
- Ensuring consistent understanding across roles
- Reviewing and improving data governance frameworks

---

### 2. Data Curators & Mappers (Blue)
**Purpose:** Transform, map, structure, and align data so it's usable and meets industry/channel and consumer expectations and standards.

**Typical responsibilities:**
- Mapping data to industry taxonomies
- Aligning data structures across systems
- Preparing curated datasets
- Supporting enrichment for online sales or external platforms

---

### 3. Data Producers (Pink)
**Purpose:** Create, enrich, update, and maintain data in source systems.

**Typical responsibilities:**
- Filling required attributes
- Checking completeness
- Correcting issues before publishing
- Ensuring timely delivery of data for onboarding

---

### 4. Data Managers (Yellow)
**Purpose:** Coordinate activities across teams, facilitate handovers, ensure processes run smoothly, and make sure people talk to each other.

**Typical responsibilities:**
- Managing workflow dependencies
- Coordinating between producers, consumers, and governance
- Communicating deadlines and risks
- Ensuring required data flows at the right time

---

### 5. Data Consumers (Pink)
**Purpose:** Use product data to make business, operational, or customer-facing decisions.

**Typical responsibilities:**
- Checking readiness for sale or distribution
- Using data in customer conversations
- Preparing applications, content, or documentation
- Performing technical or commercial evaluations

---

### 6. Leadership / Sponsors (Gray)
**Purpose:** They set strategic direction, approve priorities, allocate resources, and measure the success of product data initiatives. They care about outcomes, not features.

**Typical responsibilities:**
- Setting strategic priorities
- Ensuring alignment across divisions
- Supporting and approving investments
- Evaluating the business impact
- Sponsoring cross-functional improvement initiatives
- Ensuring teams have mandate
- Tracking metrics / following KPIs

---

### 7. System / Platform Owners (Gray)
**Purpose:** Ensure that the platform operates reliably, securely, and efficiently, while evolving in line with business needs and technical strategy.

**Typical responsibilities:**
- Managing platform lifecycle, upgrades, and performance
- Defining platform standards and guardrails
- Prioritizing incidents, improvements, and technical debt
- Coordinating across IT, vendors, and product teams
- Ensuring cost, security, and compliance requirements are met

---
