## Agenda/Goals

- **Introduction:** We welcome, introduce ourselves and talk briefly about why we are here and why it is important for us to talk to them. We also ask them if we can record the conversation and assure them that everything will be treated anonymously, will only be seen by the Delivery Partner and statements in the presentations will be anonymized.

- Mention why we as Delivery Partner are here, what is our role and what do we want to achieve
  - Identify main needs and jobs to be done by future control center users
  - Ensure the features built in the control center can support relevant processes for the users

## Questions

### A. Warm-up & Context (5')

| Question | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| Could you briefly describe your **role** and your connection to product data? | |
| What are you ultimately **accountable** for when it comes to product information shown to customers? | |

---

### B. Understanding Their Job (10')

| Question | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| How do you currently **interact** with product data today? | |
| When you work with product data, what **outcomes** are you trying to achieve? | |
| When you think about your role, what are the key **decisions** you need to make using product data? What are the most important and time-sensitive decisions? | |
| What **information** is most critical for you to have about a product? | |

---

### C. Pain Points & Challenges (10')

| Question | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| What is most **frustrating** when working with product data today? | |
| Can you recall a **recent situation** where XY (e.g., missing or unclear data) slowed you down or caused rework? | |
| Which **dependencies** or **bottlenecks** slow you down when preparing products for market readiness? | |
| Where do you lack **transparency** today — for example, knowing what has changed, where data comes from, or who owns it? | |
| Are there decisions or tasks you make today that you wish were **easier**, **faster**, or more **automated**? | |
| Are there **insights** that you currently don't have but would significantly help your role? | |
| If you could eliminate one recurring pain point with product data, what would it be? | |

---

### D. Control Center Expectations & Value (10')

| Question | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| What are the first things that come to your mind when you think of the control center? | |
| Based on what you've seen or heard, what do you expect the control center to help you with? | |
| If the control center existed last year, what job would it have helped you complete faster, more accurately, or with less stress? | |
| Imagine opening the control center in the morning — what is the **first thing** you would want it to tell you? | |
| What decisions would you like to make more confidently with the help of the control center? | |
| What are 2–3 things you wish the control center could do that you currently cannot do at all (or cannot do efficiently)? | |
| How **often** do you realistically see yourself **using** the control center, and in which situations? | |

---

### E. Value & Impact (5')

| Question | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| If we solved your top two issues, what would change in your day-to-day work or in **KPIs**? | |
| **Who else** do you think would benefit from the control center, and why? | |

---

### F. Wrap-up (2 minutes)

| Question | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| If I was a wizard and could fulfil any wish for you (regarding product management), what would you wish for? | |
| Is there anything we didn't cover that you think is important? | |

---

### G. End

> Thank you for the interview and your time and give a perspective on what's next and where we go from here.
